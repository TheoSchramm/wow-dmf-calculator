Darkmoon Faire Turn-ins

Finds the cheapest way to earn Darkmoon Faire Prize Tickets, using the auction house prices from your own Auctionator scans.

1. Load your prices

Scan the auction house in game with the Auctionator addon, then type /reload. Then load its file here.

2. Choose your reward

3. Buy and turn in

Follow the steps in order: each turn-in raises your Darkmoon Faire reputation, and the cheaper quests close as it rises. Every turn-in is listed below for reference.

Prices are Auctionator's last seen minimum buyout. WoW saves them when you log out or type /reload.
